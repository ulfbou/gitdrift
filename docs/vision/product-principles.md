# Product principles

## Purpose

These principles guide design and implementation decisions when detailed specifications do not yet determine the answer. Specifications and accepted decisions remain more specific authority.

## Evidence before judgment

Gitdrift constructs traceable evidence and bounded cases before applying judgment. Findings and judgments remain distinguishable.

## Semantic preservation over byte identity

Byte, patch, and size changes are evidence and selection signals. Preservation, weakening, contradiction, and regression are evaluated through semantic facts, obligations, authority, and completeness.

## Uncertainty is valid output

Unresolved, insufficient-evidence, partial, unavailable, and unsupported outcomes are legitimate machine-readable results. The system does not invent certainty to complete a workflow.

## Counterevidence is mandatory

A possible-regression case actively represents alternatives, replacement, supersession, equivalent or stronger representation, lineage ambiguity, scope limits, and other material counterevidence.

## Read-only core

Repository acquisition and analysis do not mutate analyzed state. Explicit output sinks are downstream and outside repository evidence unless deliberately analyzed later.

## Structured output is the product

Canonical native JSON owns serialized meaning. Human text, Markdown, SARIF, JSON Lines, and DX are projections, interoperability formats, streams, or transports.

## Provenance survives every transformation

Every derived record retains its producer, version, input evidence, method, configuration, confidence class, limitations, and applicable completeness.

## Selectors are not verdicts

Churn, size, transition count, branch count, overlap, fragmentation, pressure, and impurity may prioritize analysis. They do not establish regression, risk, authority, or poor design.

## Authority is explicit

Authority is derived from declared or repository-local evidence with scope and activation. It is never inferred solely from branch names, recency, popularity, authorship, or location.

## Identity is independent of presentation

Stable identity does not depend on display order, generated sequence numbers, mutable ref names, human wording, or rendered location when immutable evidence is available.

## Partial analysis cannot masquerade as complete

Budgets, unsupported artifacts, omitted refs, failed extractors, unavailable objects, and bounded selections reduce explicit completeness dimensions.

## Evidence classes remain distinct

Observed, computed, heuristic, AI-assisted, human-supplied, and future external evidence do not silently collapse into one certainty class.

## Profiles share one kernel

State awareness, divergence, file lineage, and repository-evolution forensics compose the same domain contracts. Profile depth must not fork common semantics.

## External products remain evidence boundaries

`dx-artifacts` and `Dx.Domain` may serve as acceptance corpora and interoperability partners. They do not become Gitdrift runtime implementation dependencies.

## Compatibility is deliberate

Package, schema, configuration, extractor, and obligation-set evolution are versioned independently where their contracts differ. Unknown data and unsupported versions are handled explicitly.
